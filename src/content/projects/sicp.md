## What it is

A work-in-progress companion to *Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs* (2nd edition), the textbook behind MIT's 6.001. The site presents the book alongside my work through it.

## How it works

The book is typeset with TeX and published as HTML; exercises are written in Scheme. The repository also carries the complete code from the second edition and MIT's sample programming assignments (problem sets) for reference.
